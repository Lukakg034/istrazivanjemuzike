# MEC-B01

First substantial pass on the Middle East and Central Asia file. Four seed records were already present (Gülpembe, Nassam alayna el-hawa, Chakrulo, Suliko). This pass added 11 more. Batch total: 15. File total: 15.

Verification in the batch: 7 Verified, 8 Partially verified.

## Coverage

Turkey 5, Israel 3, Lebanon 2, Georgia 2, Iran 2, Azerbaijan 1.

Included: an Istanbul türkü, an aşık song, Turkish pop, Persian tasnif and Iranian folk-rock, Yemenite Jewish and Hasidic songs in Hebrew, a Lebanese adaptation of the Aranjuez theme, Georgian polyphony already in the file, and the shared Sarı gəlin melody filed once under Azerbaijan.

Sarı gəlin is one record. Armenia, Turkey, Iran, and Georgia are in secondary associations because the sources disagree on origin. Asaf Zeynallı’s early-1930s piano arrangement is why a piano version is marked Yes.

Years given only in the Iranian solar calendar (مرغ سحر, گل یخ) were left blank rather than converted.

## Left out

Nassam alayna el-hawa was already in the file; it was written for a film and was not duplicated. The Voyager Golden Record’s Azerbaijani bagpipe track has no piece title in the NASA list.

Turkish, Persian, Azerbaijani, English, and Arabic Wikipedia, the National Library of Israel, the Hebrew University Jewish Music Research Centre, UNESCO’s Georgian polyphony entry, NASA’s Voyager list, Ofra Haza’s official site, Antiwar Songs, and Voice of Lebanon News.
