# OCE-B01

Oceania. File total: **122** (4 Verified, 118 Partially verified). Target of 80+ is met. Two duplicates were skipped on import: *Pōkarekare Ana* (already OCE-0002) and *Aloha ʻOe* (already NAM-0023).

## What is in the file

Australia: *Waltzing Matilda* and the APRA Top 30, including *Treaty* (Yothu Yindi). New Zealand: *Pōkarekare Ana* and the Nature's Best lists, with the three-title cap applied (Crowded House, Split Enz, Dave Dobbyn, and several other bands). Fiji: *Isa Lei* (origin also claimed for Tonga; year left empty). Hawaiʻi, filed as United States with region Polynesia: *He Mele Lāhui Hawaiʻi*, *Hawaiʻi Ponoʻī*, *Ka Wiliwili Wai*. Genre Fijian traditional is a local name not yet in the taxonomy list.

## Sources

APRA Top 30 page and the Debbie Kruger APRA PDF for the Australian top ten; Nature's Best and Nature's Best 3; the Treaty song article plus NFSA and ASO; Wikipedia and Fiji Times for *Isa Lei*; Library of Congress and Hawaiʻi State Archives for the Hawaiian songs.

## Gaps

Papua New Guinea, Samoa, Tonga as a primary country, Micronesia, Solomon Islands, Vanuatu, and most of Melanesia beyond the one Fijian song. Most rows are popular songs taken from list pages, so style is usually Pop rather than a finer local genre. Year conflicts (Husband House, Can't Get Enough) were left blank.
