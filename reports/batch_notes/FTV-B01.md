# FTV-B01

Film, television, animation and musical-theatre seeds. File total after this batch: **32** (17 Verified, 15 Partially verified). Target was 100+.

## What is in the file

Hollywood and UK scores (Williams, Barry, Derbyshire/Grainer), Italian cinema (Morricone, Rota), Japanese film and anime (Ifukube, Hisaishi, Kimura, Takahashi), French film songs (Tiersen) and a French musical (Schönberg), Chinese/Taiwan/US film (Tan Dun), Brazilian film songs from *Orfeu Negro*, one Malian score (Salif Keita, *Yeelen*), Disney songs from *The Lion King*, a Soviet cartoon song (Shainsky), and one UK instrumental cue from *Slumdog Millionaire*. South Asian film songs were not added.

## Sources

Wikipedia film, soundtrack and song articles, plus MusicBrainz, VGMdb, Oricon and Wise Music pages opened for the records that cite them. Year is stored only when one of those pages states it.

## Gaps

European cinema beyond Italy and France, most of African and Latin American cinema, television themes other than *Doctor Who*, anime openings other than *A Cruel Angel's Thesis*, Disney and Soviet animation beyond the few titles above, and Broadway/West End beyond *Wicked* and *Les Misérables*. The Oscar score list was not imported because the page does not state country.
