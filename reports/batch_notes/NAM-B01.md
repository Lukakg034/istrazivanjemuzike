Broadway, musicals, and Western art music were left for their own files. Songs written for film were left for `film_tv_animation.csv` (examples held out: *Mon pays*, *Fight the Power*, *When Doves Cry*). `piano_arrangement_potential` is `Not assessed` on every row. `audio_url` was not collected.

Nine records (NAM-0001 .. NAM-0009) were already in the file before the rest of this batch was imported. Three later candidates were skipped as duplicates: *Maple Leaf Rag*, *The Entertainer*, and *This Land Is Your Land*. *La Bamba* was imported twice (`NAM-0005` El Jarocho, `NAM-0026` Traditional). The later row was later dropped; `NAM-0005` remains. File now **120**.

## Verification

Verified means an authoritative page and a second source agree on title, attribution, country, and year. Partially verified rows have a real conflict the sources do not settle: release day, writer credit, or traditional versus named-arranger credit. No year was stored when the sources disagreed on the year (*Volver, volver*, *Frijolero*, *Straight Outta Compton*, *Electric Pow Wow Drum*).

## Countries and styles

United States covers blues, boogie-woogie, ragtime, jazz, gospel, country, bluegrass, folk, soul, R&B, funk, disco, rock, punk, metal, pop, hip-hop, Chicago house, Detroit techno, Cajun, zydeco, and Hawaiian repertoire (country United States; region Polynesia on the Hawaiian rows).

Mexico covers mariachi, ranchera, bolero, corrido, norteño, son jarocho, son istmeño, huapango, cumbia, and rock en español.

Canada covers English-language folk and rock, Québécois chanson, and Indigenous work (Buffy Sainte-Marie, A Tribe Called Red, Willie Dunn).
