## Countries

Democratic Republic of the Congo, South Africa, Ethiopia, Nigeria, Egypt, Mali, Cameroon, Algeria, Kenya, Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal, Uganda, Benin, Burundi, Zimbabwe, Cabo Verde, Gabon, Tanzania (shared with Kenya on Malaika), Angola, Ghana (Yaa Amponsah, already present), Guinea, Madagascar, Rwanda, Tunisia.

Regions: North Africa, West Africa, Central Africa, Horn of Africa, East Africa, Southern Africa, Indian Ocean. Africa was not filed as one genre.

## What was covered

- North Africa: Egyptian tarab (Umm Kulthum, Abdel Halim Hafez, Sayed Darwish), Amr Diab, Tunisian Kelmti Horra, Algerian raï (Khaled, Cheb Hasni) and chaabi (Ya Rayah).
- West Africa: Afrobeat, highlife, jùjú, mbalax, Dakar Afro-Cuban band music, morna, Mandingue/Wassoulou, desert blues, Afrobeats (Ojuelegba).
- Central Africa: Congolese rumba, ndombolo, soukous-era Kinshasa songs, makossa, and other Cameroonian and Gabonese songs from the Daily Nation list.
- Horn and East: Ethio-jazz and Ethiopian popular song, benga (Kiseru), Kenyan, Tanzanian, Ugandan and Rwandan songs.
- Southern Africa: township and jazz (Mannenberg, Grazing in the Grass, kwela), isicathamiya, chimurenga, semba, reggae, house (Jerusalema).
- Indian Ocean: one salegy title (Samy Mandeha Samy Mitady).

Many Kinshasa, South African pop and East African titles come from a single Daily Nation list (21 June 2021) and have no composer and often no year. Those stay partially verified. Years were left empty when sources disagreed (Joromi 1965 vs 1969; Pata Pata 1967 vs 1959/1969; Jambo Bwana 1980 vs 1982) or when only a compilation listed the title.
