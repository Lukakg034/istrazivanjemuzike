# EUR-B03

Continental Europe after the UK/Ireland batch. The file already held six EUR-B03 seeds (La Vie en rose, Lili Marleen, Nel blu dipinto di blu, Grândola Vila Morena, Korobeiniki, Coimbra). This pass added 25 more. Batch total: 31. File total: 55.

Verification in the batch: 24 Verified, 7 Partially verified.

## Coverage

Spain 6, France 4, Germany 3, Italy 3, Portugal 3, Russia 2, Belgium 2, and one each for Norway, Sweden, Austria, Hungary, Ukraine, Czechia, Poland, and the Netherlands.

Nova cançó is used as a genre name for Serrat and Llach. It was not in the taxonomy file; it is a local style name, not a substitute for “world music.”

## Left out on purpose

Podmoskovnye vechera was written for a film. Hej, sokoły has a possible pre-1900 stage attribution and was not filed. Shchedryk’s Leontovych arrangement belongs with Western art music.

## Sources

French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Czech, Polish, Dutch, Ukrainian, and Russian Wikipedia, plus SACEM, INA, Offizielle Deutsche Charts, the official ABBA site, UK Official Charts, Eurovision.com, the Paco de Lucía Foundation, and NPO Radio 2.

## Still open

Estonia, Latvia, and Lithuania have no records. Finland, Denmark, Iceland, Slovakia, Slovenia’s non-Balkan file, Switzerland, Luxembourg, Belarus, and most of the Russian and Ukrainian repertory are still thin or empty. No country here is fully researched.
