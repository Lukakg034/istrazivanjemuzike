# VGM-B01

Video-game themes. File total: **10** (5 Verified, 5 Partially verified). Target was 80+.

## What is in the file

All ten rows are Japanese: Koji Kondo (*Super Mario Bros.* ground theme already present, plus the Zelda overworld theme), Hirokazu Tanaka (Tetris Type A), three Nobuo Uematsu cues from *Final Fantasy*, Kow Otani's *Shadow of the Colossus* soundtrack, and three Yasunori Mitsuda cues from *Chrono Trigger* (Corridors of Time, Schala's Theme, To Far Away Times). The three-title cap stopped further Uematsu and Kondo titles.

## Sources

Wikipedia game and soundtrack articles, VGMPF, MusicBrainz and the Nintendo interview cited on the Zelda row. Chrono Trigger cue credits follow the soundtrack table on the Music of Chrono Trigger page: blank Music cells are Mitsuda; cells marked Uematsu were not added.

## Gaps

PlayStation, PC, indie, chiptune beyond Tetris, and any non-Japanese game. Country was not stored for titles whose opened pages did not state it, which is why several well-known themes were left out.
