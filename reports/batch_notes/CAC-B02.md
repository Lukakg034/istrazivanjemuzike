# CAC-B02

Caribbean and Central America beyond the Cuban CAC-B01 set. Batch total: **10** (4 Verified, 6 Partially verified). Target was 40+.

## What is in the batch

Jamaica: *No Woman, No Cry* (already in the file before this pass). Haiti: *Ti Carole* (already present), *New York City* (Tabou Combo). Trinidad and Tobago: *Sugar Bum Bum* (Lord Kitchener; melody authorship disputed). Panama: *El gran varón* (Willie Colón; written by Omar Alfanno). Belize: *Weyu Larigi Weyu* and *Ámuñegü* (Andy Palacio) and *Ayó Da* (Paul Nabor; year left empty). Dominican Republic: *Ojalá Que Llueva Café* and *Ángel para una Tambora* (Juan Luis Guerra).

## Sources

Wikipedia song and album articles, Trinidad Express, swisscharts, taboucombo.com, the Garifuna Collective Bandcamp page, and the Spanish Wikipedia song page that names Guerra as author of *Ojalá Que Llueva Café*.

## Gaps

Puerto Rico as a primary country, most of the Dominican and Jamaican repertoires, the French Antilles, and Central America beyond Belize and the one Panamanian salsa. Cuba stays in CAC-B01 and was not extended here.
